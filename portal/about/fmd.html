Functions of the Major Divisions:

Extension Services performs the third major function of the University. community through the conduct of technology promotion; non-formal education and training; technology demonstration; advisory services; and other community development activities for farmers, fisherfolks, women and children, persons with disabilities, out-of-school youths, disadvantaged families and/or households, workers, migrants, and other clients.
The Extension Services performs the overall coordinate function. It is tasked to perform the major extension activity of the University and to assist in the conceptualization, implementation, evaluation, and monitoring of extension programs, projects, and activities of the different colleges, campuses, and other extension implementing units.
